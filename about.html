About

BittrByte exists to build practical answers to the harms created by modern technology.

Digital platforms now evolve faster than parents, schools, regulators, and individuals can respond. The result is a growing gap between the speed at which technology creates new behavioural problems and the speed at which society can address them.

BittrByte was founded to close that gap.

SKYWALL™ is the first expression of that mission: a device-level system designed to help people create stronger digital boundaries before harm, distraction, or impulse takes hold.

Founder story

From breadboard to shipping SKYWALL.

The most valuable things aren't found by people looking for them. They're found by people who can't live without them.

For years, I struggled with digital addiction. Walking away from technology gave me freedom, but it was never a real solution. In the modern world, digital participation is essential for work, education and everyday life.

Our devices are always with us. They are our alarm clocks, entertainment, social lives, fitness trackers, payment systems and proof of identity. The source of the problem is also something we cannot live without.

When returning to the digital world became necessary to pursue my lifelong ambition of designing aircraft, I faced a choice between that dream and the freedom I had found.

Instead of giving up on that dream, I decided to build another way.

I ordered some computer components online and began working on something that could allow me to pursue my career and live a normal life without giving up that freedom.

That decision became SKYWALL.

It is now my mission to ensure that millions of others who have suffered in silence do not have to choose between their freedom and their future.

Beni Ayuk — Founder & CEO. Former BAE Systems airframe engineer.
