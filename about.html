About

BittrByte exists to build practical answers to the harms created by modern technology.

Digital platforms evolve faster than parents, schools, regulators, and individuals can respond. BittrByte was founded to close that gap.

We're building a device company — hardware running SKYWALL™ OS, a downloadable operating system, and a developer community to refine it before public release.

SKYWALL is the first expression of that mission: a device-level system designed to help people create stronger digital boundaries before harm, distraction, or impulse takes hold.
