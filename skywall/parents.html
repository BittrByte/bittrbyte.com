Default overnight social media curfew for 16- and 17-year-olds

“Hey SKYWALL, block social media apps between midnight and 6am, and only let me change this restriction at the library between 9am and 10am.”

Teenagers can opt out of the government’s proposed default social media curfew for 16 and 17-year-olds, making it little more than a recommendation.

Under-16 social media accounts to be banned

Parents want better choices, not fewer choices.

Prime Minister Keir Starmer announced a ban on social media accounts for children under 16. Blanket bans remove options — they don’t give families tools that fit real life.
