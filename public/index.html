The Problem

Digital devices cannot tell commitment from impulse.

The same person who sets a limit can remove it moments later. When temptation arrives, the device obeys the impulse just as readily as it obeyed the promise. That is the architecture that needs to change.

The Solution

SKYWALL: restrictions enforced by reality, not willpower.

2 patents filed

Other tools attempt to block access. SKYWALL controls something deeper: when you're allowed to change the rule you set. You set what to limit. You also set what must happen before that limit can be changed: go to a place, or wait until a moment, both chosen from your own life. Until that condition is met, edits and deletion are blocked. The way out is a real-world action, not a password. That is what makes the limit strong enough to hold, but still releasable on terms chosen in advance.

OS-level

Built into the device, not layered on top as a deletable app.

User-authored

The user sets both the limit and the release condition.

Real-world release

The way out is a place or a moment — not a password.
