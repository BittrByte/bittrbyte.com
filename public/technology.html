The SKYWALL Technology SKYWALL

SKYWALL is a family of patent-pending technologies designed to make digital restrictions stronger, more flexible and easier to live with.

01

User-Defined Release Conditions

Users decide what to restrict and define the real-world conditions under which their decisions may be changed.

  • No recurring timers to reset.
  • No external hardware or accountability partner required.
  • Restrictions remain reversible under preselected conditions.

02

Child-Authored Policies

Children can voluntarily establish additional digital restrictions within their parental-control environment.

  • Encourages personal responsibility.
  • Supports individually chosen boundaries.
  • Complements existing parental restrictions.

03

Ecosystem-Wide Rules

Designed to extend digital restrictions across compatible devices within an ecosystem.

  • Consistent boundaries across devices.
  • Less repetitive configuration.
  • Greater flexibility for families and individuals.

04

Parental Self-Binding

Parents can define when and where they are permitted to modify restrictions they establish for their children.

  • Reduces impulsive exceptions.
  • Protects decisions made in advance.
  • Makes parental controls easier to uphold consistently.
