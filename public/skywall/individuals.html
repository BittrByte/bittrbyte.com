Digital devices cannot tell commitment from impulse.

The Solution

SKYWALL™: restrictions enforced by reality, not willpower.

2 patents filed

You set what to limit. You also set what must happen before that limit can be changed: go to a place, or wait until a moment. Until that condition is met, edits and deletion are blocked. The way out is a real-world action, not a password.
