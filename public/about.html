Our Mission

Our goal is not to return the world to what it was in the 80s or 90s, but to make possible a better life than we could have had even then.

Long before digital addiction, people struggled with alcohol, prescription medication and gambling. Addressing the harms of digital technology is therefore only the beginning of what we hope to achieve.

As technology becomes increasingly integrated into how we pay, shop and access physical spaces, we envision SKYWALL allowing people to voluntarily restrict not only their digital experiences, but also their purchases and activities in the physical world. Someone could, for example, make a commitment in advance that prevents them from purchasing alcohol or entering a casino.

This is the future we want to build: one in which technology does more than solve the problems it has created, but also helps us overcome those that existed long before it, fulfilling its original promise to make our lives better.
