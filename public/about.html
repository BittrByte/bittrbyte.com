Our Mission

Our goal is not to take us back to the 80s or 90s, but to use technology to make life better than it has ever been.

The world before smartphones was not free from addiction. We want to address the problems digital technology has introduced while using its capabilities to help tackle those that existed long before it.

SKYWALL begins with digital boundaries, but our vision extends beyond the screen. As our devices increasingly handle payments and access, we envision people being able to prevent their own alcohol purchases or entry to casinos, just as they can restrict unwanted digital content.

By enabling people to set lasting boundaries around what they can buy and access, technology could give them new ways to overcome old problems. That is the future we want to build.
