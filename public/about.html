Our Mission

Our goal is not to return the world to what it was in the 80s or 90s, but to fulfil the promise of digital technology: to make our lives easier, expand our possibilities and give us greater freedom over how we live.

As our devices become gateways to more of everyday life, from communication and entertainment to payments, transport and access, that ambition extends beyond the screen. SKYWALL is our first step towards a future in which people can decide how technology should serve them and trust their devices to uphold those decisions, giving them more control over their lives than they had before digital technology existed.
