Our Mission

Our goal is not to take us back to the 80s or 90s, but to use technology to make life better than it was then.

Removing the harms of digital addiction would be progress, but people would still face the addictions that existed before smartphones, including alcohol and gambling. We believe digital technology can help with those too.

SKYWALL allows people to put unwanted digital experiences out of reach. As payments and entry to physical places increasingly depend on digital systems, our vision is to extend that same choice beyond the screen: enabling people to restrict their own alcohol purchases or casino entry through boundaries they set in advance.

We want to use the growing role of technology in everyday life to make stronger personal boundaries possible, helping people tackle both the problems it has introduced and those that existed long before it.
