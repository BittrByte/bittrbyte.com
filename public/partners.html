Why partner with BittrByte?

BittrByte develops OS-level technologies for digital self-control and parental control. We offer our methods through licensing, technical integration and joint development with device manufacturers and digital safeguarding providers.

For device manufacturers

  • Differentiate your devices with stronger, native digital controls.
  • Give users and families meaningful choices within your existing ecosystem.
  • Integrate our methods with your platform and safeguarding capabilities.

For digital safeguarding providers

  • Combine your on-device detection with our restriction and enforcement methods.
  • Let users and parents define lasting boundaries around your protection.
  • Work together to bring complementary technologies to more devices.
